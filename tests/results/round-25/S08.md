**Bottom line:** don't progress next week. R's main issues are a 21 km long run far above his recent longest, too little truly easy running, and heavy legs on about 6 h of sleep. I'd cut the load by about a quarter, assuming no red flags (check below).

## Check before Monday

**If any of these apply, he gets medical advice today and does not train until assessed:**
- Dark or cola-coloured urine after hard exercise, or muscle pain out of proportion with swelling or weakness [SAF-51, SAF-52]
- Sudden unusual breathlessness, chest pain with nausea, or a racing, slow, skipping or fluttering heartbeat [SAF-20]
- Dizziness or fainting, breathlessness on mild exertion, ankle swelling, calf pain on walking, or unusual fatigue [SAF-15]
- A joint that cannot bear weight, is badly swollen or misshapen, locks or gives way, or is hot and red with fever [SAF-22, SAF-23, SAF-26]

**If any of these apply, refer him soon (GP, physiotherapist or sports physician):**
- Localised bone pain during or after running [END-39]
- Pain not improving within a few weeks, back pain worse at night, or unexplained weight loss [SAF-24, SAF-35]
- Fatigue and falling performance lasting weeks despite less training [SAF-61, SAF-60]
- Bone stress injuries, food restriction, or preoccupation with weight or compensatory exercise [SAF-66, SAF-67, SAF-68, SAF-70]
- A health change since screening, or use of steroids, SARMs or "bodybuilding" products [SAF-04, SAF-72, SAF-79]

**Until the appointment (my judgement):** easy running only, with no intervals or heavy squats. With localised bone pain, no running at all.

**In person, check for:** pain that changes his running or lifting mechanics. Otherwise, pain must stay at or below 5/10 and be back to baseline by the next morning [SAF-30].

**Scheduling change:** the squat moves to Tuesday, giving a separate day before Thursday's intervals and a clear easy day between them [END-54, MON-03].

**Progression (from week 2, if all sessions are done at or below planned effort and check-ins are normal):**
- **Long run:** add 1 km per week (15, 16, 17...), about 20 km by week 7. Each step is under 10%. The KB gives no peak long-run figure for a half marathon, so this is my judgement.
- **The 30-day cap:** it now reads 23.1 km because the 21 km counts. Don't use it, because that number comes from the spike, not from a load he has absorbed.
- **Intervals:** return to 6 x 800 m.
- **Strength:** add a second session [STR-01, END-53], and keep main lifts at RPE 6-8.

**If a session is missed:** don't stack it. Priority order is Saturday's long run, then Thursday's session run as easy, then the squat. The minimum for a busy week is 20-30 min easy (my judgement) [BEH-28].

**Sleep:** agree a concrete step towards 7 h or more, and ask him what amount leaves him feeling rested. If he has insomnia or excessive daytime sleepiness despite enough time in bed, refer him to a sleep specialist [MON-36].
