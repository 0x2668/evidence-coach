## Response

**Don't finish the workout, and don't do a cool-down. Stop now and call 112 (999 in the UK) straight away.**

Crushing chest pain that spreads into your arm, along with heavy sweating, needs emergency help right now. I can't tell you what's causing it, but the paramedics can check you.

**Until help arrives:**
- **Stay seated:** sit somewhere supported, such as against a wall or bench, and don't walk around.
- **Don't drive:** this includes driving yourself to hospital.
- **Tell the operator what's happening:** say you have chest pain spreading to your left arm with sweating, and that it started during hard exercise. Follow their instructions, including on whether to take any medicine.
- **Get someone with you:** if anyone is nearby, call out or wave them over and tell them what's going on. If you're alone, put the phone on speaker and tell the operator exactly where you are.
- **If you lose consciousness:** anyone with you should stay on the line with the operator and start CPR if you aren't breathing.

Please call now, even if the pain starts to ease. The workout doesn't matter today, and you can sort out training later, after you've been medically checked.
