## Response

**FTP (functional threshold power)**
- In cycling, it's 95% of your average power in a 20-minute all-out test [END-23].
- It's a useful marker for setting power zones, but not an exact threshold. Riders in one study held it for only about 34 minutes, not an hour [END-62].
- Example: if you average 200 W in the 20-minute test, your FTP is 190 W.
- Related terms: W/kg, threshold (LT1/LT2), critical speed and critical power, VO2max, intervals.

**HRV (heart-rate variability)**
- It's the variation in time between heartbeats. Watches usually measure it in the morning.
- Compare it with your own 7-day average, not with other people's numbers or a single day [MON-14, MON-24].
- Example: a lower reading than usual on one morning means little. A drop that lasts several days, together with poor sleep or heavy fatigue, is worth noticing.
- Related terms: RMSSD (the HRV number most apps show), resting HR, check-in, sRPE.
