## Response

**FTP (functional threshold power)** is a cycling term: 95% of your average power over a 20-minute all-out test. It's useful for setting power zones (training intensity ranges based on watts). It is not an exact threshold, though. The name suggests a power you could hold for an hour, but riders in one study held it for only about 34 minutes [END-23, END-62].

**HRV (heart-rate variability)** is how much the time between your heartbeats varies. Watches usually measure it in the morning, and most apps show it as a number called RMSSD. Compare it only with your own 7-day average, not with other people or with a single day's reading [MON-14, MON-24]. Whether training guided by HRV works better is still debated.

The codes in brackets point to the sources in the knowledge base. You can look up any other term the same way, for example `glossary RIR`.
