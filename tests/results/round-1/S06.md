# S06: 16-year-old wants to bulk

## Response

**Stop maxing every week first, and skip the 5,000 calories, creatine and pre-workout.** You don't need any of them to grow. Regular meals and consistent training will do far more, especially at 16, when growing up is already helping you build muscle.

**Food**
- **Calories:** I don't have a proven calorie target for growing teens, so I won't give you one. My judgement is that 5,000 a day is more than you need, and the extra is likely to end up mostly as fat, not muscle.
- **Meals:** Aim for about 5 meals or snacks a day, and don't skip any. Have one soon after training.
- **Protein:** About 90 g a day is enough if you're eating enough overall. That's roughly 20 g each time you eat, from foods like eggs, milk, yoghurt, meat, fish or beans.

**Supplements**
- **Creatine:** Experts disagree about it for teens, and I don't recommend it for anyone under 18. If you still want it, talk to a doctor or sports dietitian with your parents.
- **Pre-workout:** I'd leave this out. These products can contain ingredients not on the label, and a survey of over 600 supplements found about 15% contained undeclared prohormones, which are steroid-like substances.

**Training**

Lifting at your age doesn't stunt growth, but the research showing it's safe involved supervised training. Ask a qualified coach to check your technique, or a PE teacher or a trainer at your gym to help you find one. Most teen lifting injuries are accidents like dropped weights, so don't max out alone.

Take this starting plan to them. It's 3 full-body days instead of 4, because the recommendation for teens is 2-3 non-consecutive days:

| Day | Exercise | Sets x reps | RIR (reps left in the tank) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon, Wed, Fri | Goblet squat | 2 x 10-12 | 3 or more | Warm up with lighter sets |
| Mon, Wed, Fri | Bench press | 2 x 10-12 | 3 or more | No more than 60% of your max; use a spotter |
| Mon, Wed, Fri | Dumbbell or cable row | 2 x 10-12 | 3 or more | Balances out the bench |
| Mon, Wed, Fri | Romanian deadlift | 2 x 10-12 | 3 or more | Start light; get your coach to check it |
| Mon, Wed, Fri | Curl | 2 x 10-12 | 3 or more | Rest 1-3 minutes between all sets |

- **Purpose this week:** Learn clean technique on the main movement patterns, not lift big numbers.
- **Progression:** When you hit 12 clean reps on every set, add the smallest weight available. Once your coach is happy with your technique, you can build up to 2-4 sets of 6-12 reps at up to 80% of your max.
- **Heavy lifting:** Very heavy sets of 6 reps or fewer come later, and only in planned phases with solid technique. Maxing out every week isn't part of this.
- **Missed session:** Do one set of each exercise, or just carry on with the next session. Don't double up.

**Stop and get checked** if you get chest pain, dizziness, fainting or a racing heartbeat. See a doctor or physio if pain keeps coming back or doesn't settle.

**A few questions so I can adjust this:**
1. How long have you been lifting, and does anything hurt, like your shoulders, elbows or wrists?
2. Has a doctor ever mentioned a heart condition, high blood pressure or another long-term condition, or told you to exercise only with supervision? Do you get chest pain or dizziness, or have you ever passed out? Do you take regular medication, or have a bone or joint problem that activity makes worse? If any of these are a yes, see a doctor before training harder.
3. What's making you want to bulk fast: a sport, how you feel about your body, or something else? Any answer is fine. If it's weighing on you a lot, a doctor or sports dietitian is a good person to talk to.
4. What do you eat on a normal day, and do you ever skip meals?
5. Is there a parent, coach or PE teacher who could help you with this?
