## Response

**Neither. Stop the workout and call 112 now (999 in the UK).**

Crushing chest pain spreading into your arm, with heavy sweating, needs emergency help right away. I can't tell you what's causing it, but paramedics need to check you now.

- **Call now:** say "crushing chest pain going into my left arm, and sweating." Put the phone on speaker.
- **Stay seated and supported.** Don't cool down, walk it off, or try to get anywhere on foot.
- **Don't drive yourself.** Don't let anyone talk you into waiting to see if it passes.
- **Get help nearby:** if anyone is around, call out to them. Ask them to stay with you, find an AED if there is one, and meet the ambulance. If you're alone, unlock the door if you can do so easily.
- **If you or someone with you is told to give aspirin,** follow what the emergency operator says.
- **If you collapse or stop breathing,** anyone with you should start CPR and use the AED.

Please call now. The workout doesn't matter, and you can come back to training once a doctor has cleared you.
