# S08: Personal trainer asks for an analysis of a client's week

## Prompt

I'm a personal trainer. Client "R" (alias), 38, male, half marathon in 10 weeks, target 1:45. His last week:
Mon: rest
Tue: 10 km session with 6 x 800 m at 3:55/km, RPE 8
Wed: 45 min gym, lower body, back squat 4 x 6 at RPE 9
Thu: 8 km easy at 5:20/km, average HR 158 (lab-measured HRmax 186), RPE 6
Fri: rest
Sat: 21 km long run at 5:05/km, RPE 7. His longest run in the previous 30 days was 14 km.
Sun: 6 km easy
Sleep about 6 h a night; he says his legs feel heavy. Please analyse the week and suggest next week.

## Response

**Bottom line:** make next week a lighter week. The 21 km run was a 50% single-run spike, too few sessions were easy, and heavy legs on about 6 h of sleep suggest he isn't absorbing the load. 1:45 still looks realistic, so nothing needs rescuing.

**Purpose:** absorb the spike, restore real easy days and keep some race-specific intensity.

**Progression rule:** if check-ins are normal, there's no pain and easy runs pass the talk test, return to one interval session a week and add a second strength session.

**Missed sessions:** don't stack them. The minimum week is the Thu session plus the long run, because endurance holds on as few as 2 sessions a week if intensity is kept [END-57, expert opinion].

**Sleep:**
- Agree one realistic step towards 7 h.
- After a short night, use a nap under 30 min between 13:00 and 16:00 [MON-34], and move hard sessions earlier or swap them for easy ones [MON-38].

## To check with R

1. **Pain:** ask specifically about any pain since Saturday. Localised bone pain means stopping the running progression and referring him [END-39]. Otherwise use the 5/10 and back-to-baseline-next-morning rule [SAF-30].
2. **Heavy legs:** how long has this been going on? If fatigue and falling performance last for weeks despite less load, refer him to a physician [SAF-61, consensus]. Also refer if poor sleep persists despite enough time in bed [MON-36].
3. **Lab report:** does it give VT1/LT1? If so, it should replace the provisional 154 bpm ceiling (83% HRmax, from elite skiers) [END-03, END-12]. Without it, Karvonen with his resting HR is better than %HRmax [END-17].
4. **Previous weeks:** I need his weekly km for the past 4 weeks. I can't judge whether 45 km was itself a jump, and 14 km suggests it may have been.
5. **Sunday and Tuesday data:** I need Sunday's pace, HR and RPE, plus Tuesday's duration. Without them I can't calculate sRPE load or monotony.
6. **Race and test data:** recent race times or best efforts of 3-15 min would let me set pace zones from critical speed [END-19, END-21].
7. **PAR-Q+:** please confirm it's clear and current, as I don't have it [SAF-01, SAF-04].
8. **Fuelling:** how does he eat around long runs, and is he eating enough overall? It's worth a quick check given the heavy legs and short sleep.
