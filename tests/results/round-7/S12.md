**What this means**
- **Heart rate drift:** your heart rate rose 12 bpm between halves at the same pace. This is common after about 10 minutes of steady running, and it's larger in the heat. It means the run really was getting harder for your body.
- **The easy limit is approximate:** I set it at about 83% of your max, a rough band that comes from elite skiers. Your lab max makes the percentages more accurate, but it doesn't show where your own easy zone ends. If that lab test also measured your first threshold (LT1/VT1), use that heart rate instead.
- **How it felt matters too:** if you could speak in full sentences to the end, it was probably still close to easy. If talking got harder in the last 10-15 minutes, you'd moved past easy.

**Next time:** keep the talk test as your main guide and use about 157 bpm as a rough ceiling. If your heart rate climbs past that, slow down and accept the slower pace. That doesn't mean you've lost fitness, especially on a hot day.

**Check the file:** it's dated 25 October, which is a week ago rather than yesterday, so make sure it's the right run. I also only used this file, not your Strava, and it doesn't show whether the heart rate came from a wrist or chest sensor. Wrist readings are less precise.

**Two questions would firm this up:**
1. Could you still talk in full sentences during the last 10-15 minutes?
2. Was it warm, or were you tired or short on sleep that day?
