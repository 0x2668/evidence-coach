**R's last week was too hard for his recovery, mainly because of the 21 km long run. Next week I'd hold rather than build: about 36 km, one controlled quality session and a 15 km long run. Clear the red-flag questions below first, because heavy legs alone don't tell us the cause.**

Zones use the 3-zone model. The CR-10 targets for easy and steady running, and all distances and interval doses, are my judgement, because the KB gives no figures for them.

**Purpose:** absorb the spike, make easy days truly easy and restore recovery, at about 20% less running volume.

**Progression rule:**
- **If check-ins are normal, Saturday hit its target RPE and easy HR is falling:** add a short second strength session and extend the long run to about 16.5 km.
- **Long-run base:** the 10% formula now technically allows 23.1 km, because 21 km is in the 30-day window. I wouldn't use that; build from 15 km as if the spike hadn't happened (my judgement).
- **If legs are still heavy:** repeat this week.

**If sessions are missed:** don't stack them. The minimum week is Wednesday's run plus Saturday's run, plus 2 x 6 squats if he has time.

**Toward the race:**
- **Taper:** keep the last 2 weeks for it, cutting volume by about 41-60% while keeping intensity and frequency [END-50; meta-analysis].
- **Field test:** once he's fresh, test 3 efforts of about 3-15 min, such as 1500 m, 3 km and 5 km, to set pace zones from critical speed [END-19, END-21]. His 800s can't be used for this.

## Please check with R

**1. Red flags**
- **Today, and call 112 instead for tight or spreading chest pain, severe breathlessness or collapse:** has he had any of the following? If so, he stops training and gets medical advice the same day.
  - Dark or cola-coloured urine, or muscle pain out of proportion with swelling or weakness [SAF-51, SAF-52]
  - Unusual breathlessness, chest pain with nausea, or a racing, slow, skipping or fluttering heartbeat [SAF-20]
  - Dizziness or fainting, ankle swelling, calf pain on walking, or unusual fatigue [SAF-15]
  - A joint that can't bear weight, is badly swollen or misshapen, locks or gives way, or is hot and red with fever [SAF-22, SAF-23, SAF-26]
- **Soon, with GP or sports physician:** any of these needs referral, with training adapted meanwhile.
  - Localised bone pain, especially in the foot [END-39]
  - Fatigue and falling performance lasting weeks despite less training [SAF-61, SAF-60]
  - Pain not improving within a few weeks [SAF-24]
- **Fever or feeling unwell at rest:** no training [MON-45].

**2. Screening:** has he completed the PAR-Q+, and has anything changed since [SAF-01, SAF-04]?

**3. Thursday's HR:** was it from a wrist or chest sensor, and was it hot or hilly? Could he speak in full sentences?

**4. Benchmarks:** does he have recent race times or best 3-15 min efforts, and a resting HR? Resting HR would allow %HRR zones [END-17].

**5. Missing data:** what were his Tue, Wed and Sun session RPEs and durations, and which days can he train?

In person, it's also worth watching his squat depth and technique at RPE 7-8, and how he moves on Tuesday's run after the spike.
