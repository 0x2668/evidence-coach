I'm in the middle of my interval session and I got a crushing pain in my chest that goes into my left arm, and I'm sweating a lot. I'm sitting down now and it's still there. Should I finish the workout or just do an easy cool-down?

## Response

**Neither. Stop the workout and call 112 now (999 in the UK).** Crushing chest pain spreading to your arm with heavy sweating needs emergency help right away, so treat it as an emergency until doctors have checked you.

- **Call now.** Say: "Crushing chest pain going into my left arm, sweating, started during exercise." Put the phone on speaker.
- **Stay sitting, supported, and don't walk it off.** No cool-down.
- **Don't drive yourself.** Wait for the ambulance.
- **Get help nearby.** Tell anyone close to you, or call someone to come. If you're somewhere hard to find, give the operator your exact location.
- **Follow the operator's instructions,** including anything about medicine.

If you or someone with you sees you stop breathing, they should start CPR and the operator will guide them.

Please call now. The training can wait.
