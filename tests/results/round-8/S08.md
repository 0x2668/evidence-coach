## Response

**Bottom line:** the biggest concern is the 21 km long run, followed by too little easy running and only about 6 h of sleep. I'd make next week a lighter week, with a 15 km long run and easier strength work. This plan is provisional until you've checked the red flags below.

- **Purpose:** reduce fatigue and absorb the spike while keeping one quality session and heavy-ish strength work.
- **Easy-run HR:** the talk test takes priority. About 154 bpm (83% of HRmax) is only a rough ceiling [END-03]. If he has lab LT1/VT1 values, use those instead [END-12].
- **Sleep:** agree on a realistic step toward 7 h. If nights stay short, add a nap of under 30 min between 13:00 and 16:00, and do hard sessions earlier in the day [MON-34, MON-38].
- **Progression:** if the check-ins are normal and effort is on target, resume building the week after, one or two changes at a time.
    - Add about 1-2 km to the long run each week (my judgement).
    - The 21 km run technically raises his 30-day limit to about 23 km, but I wouldn't use that headroom. One spike isn't an established base, and consecutive 10% steps can still be too much [MON-12].
    - Add the second strength session when his legs feel normal.
- **Missed session:** don't make it up. The minimum week is the easy long run plus one 20-30 min easy run (my judgement) [BEH-28].
- **Up to race day:** that leaves about 8 weeks of build, then a taper of about 2 weeks. Cut volume by about 41-60% and keep intensity and frequency [END-50].

## What to check with R

1. **Medical advice today if any of these are present:**
    - Dark or cola-coloured urine after Saturday [SAF-51]
    - Muscle pain out of proportion, with swelling or weakness [SAF-52]
    - A joint that can't bear weight, is badly swollen or misshapen, locks or gives way, or is hot and red with fever [SAF-22, SAF-23, SAF-26]
    - Chest pain with nausea, sudden unusual breathlessness, or a racing, slow, skipping or fluttering heartbeat [SAF-20]
    - Dizziness or fainting, breathlessness on mild exertion, ankle swelling, calf pain on walking, or unusual fatigue [SAF-15]
2. **Stop the running progression and refer to a GP or physiotherapist if any of these are present:**
    - Localised bone pain, especially in the foot [END-39]
    - Soreness worsening or lasting beyond 5-7 days [SAF-51]
    - Pain that isn't improving within a few weeks [SAF-24]
    - Fatigue and falling performance lasting weeks despite less training [SAF-61]
3. **Any other pain:** where is it and how strong? He can continue with modifications only if it stays at or below 5/10 and is back to baseline by the next morning [SAF-30].
4. **Screening and fuelling:** has he completed the PAR-Q+ with no "yes" answers [SAF-01, SAF-04]? Is he eating and fuelling enough for this training?
5. **Context:**
    - His weekly km over the last 4-8 weeks, and whether this week was typical
