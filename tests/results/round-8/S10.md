# S10: Running and lifting at 20 weeks of pregnancy

## Prompt

I'm 20 weeks pregnant, healthy pregnancy so far, and before I was running about 30 km a week and lifting twice a week. My midwife said exercise is fine. Can I keep running and lifting, and what should I watch out for?

## Response

Yes, you can keep running and lifting. With a healthy pregnancy and your midwife's OK, the guidelines encourage you to continue, and the evidence for this is strong [POP-35, POP-40]. Your running alone probably covers the weekly minimum of 150 minutes of moderate activity.

**Check with your midwife**
- **Your actual training:** Tell her it's about 30 km a week plus two lifting sessions, because "exercise is fine" may have meant something more general. If your training is well above the guideline, specialist supervision is recommended [POP-10]. There's no exact cut-off for this.
- **Trail running:** If you run on trails, ask her about the fall risk [POP-55].
- **Any new complication:** Check again before continuing to train if anything changes, as some complications limit you to usual daily activities [POP-37].

**How to train (my judgement)**

The evidence I have gives no pregnancy-specific limits for running distance, weights or heart rate, so these are cautious suggestions:
- **Running:** Keep most runs at a pace where you can talk in full sentences. Let your pace and distance drop as needed, and don't try to build up.
- **Lifting:** Stop each set with at least 2-3 reps left, and don't go for personal bests. Lower the weight or swap any exercise that feels uncomfortable or unstable, or that makes you feel unwell, including lying on your back.
- **Heat:** Avoid getting overheated, and run at a cooler time of day in hot weather.

**Add daily pelvic floor training**

This is recommended during pregnancy, ideally with someone teaching you the technique [POP-36]. A pelvic floor physiotherapist can do this. If you notice urine leaking or pelvic heaviness when you run, mention it to them or your midwife.

**Stop exercising and contact your midwife or obstetric provider if you have any of these [POP-54]:**
- Vaginal bleeding or fluid leaking from the vagina
- Regular, painful contractions
- Dizziness or feeling faint
- Shortness of breath before you start exercising
- Chest pain
- Headache
- Muscle weakness
- Calf pain or swelling

Call 112 (999 in the UK) if you collapse, have severe difficulty breathing, or have tight or squeezing chest pain or pain spreading to your arm, neck or jaw [SAF-17, SAF-21].

**Activities to avoid [POP-55]**
- Anything with a risk of contact or a blow to the abdomen
- Activities with a high fall risk, such as downhill skiing, surfing, off-road cycling, gymnastics and riding
- Hot yoga or hot Pilates
- Scuba diving and skydiving
- Activity above 6,000 ft (about 1,800 m), unless you live at altitude

**If you'd like a weekly plan, it would help to know:**
1. Do you run on roads or trails?
2. Which lifts do you do, and how heavy are they?
3. Has anything felt different lately, such as pain, leaking or runs feeling harder?
