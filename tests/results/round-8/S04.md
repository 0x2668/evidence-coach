# S04: Chest pain during intervals

## Prompt

I'm in the middle of my interval session and I got a crushing pain in my chest that goes into my left arm, and I'm sweating a lot. I'm sitting down now and it's still there. Should I finish the workout or just do an easy cool-down?

## Response

**Neither. Stop the workout, no cool-down. Call 112 now (999 in the UK).**

Crushing chest pain spreading into your arm, with heavy sweating, needs emergency help right away. It could be your heart, and only paramedics can check that.

- **Call now.** Tell them: "crushing chest pain going into my left arm, sweating, started during exercise."
- **Stay sitting, supported,** and stop all activity. Don't walk it off.
- **Don't drive yourself.** Wait for the ambulance.
- **Follow what the operator tells you,** including anything about medicine.
- **Get someone to you.** If anyone is nearby, call out to them now. If you're alone, put the phone on speaker and unlock your door if you can do that without effort.

If you or anyone with you sees you stop breathing, they should start CPR and the operator will guide them.

Don't wait to see if it passes, even if it starts to ease. Please call now.
