## Response

**Neither. Stop the workout and call an ambulance now: 112 (EU), 999 (UK), or your local emergency number.**

Crushing chest pain that spreads to your arm, with heavy sweating, is an emergency until a doctor says otherwise. Don't do a cool-down, and don't wait to see if it passes.

1. **Call now**, before reading anything else. Say: "Crushing chest pain spreading to my left arm, sweating, started during exercise." Put the phone on speaker.
2. **Stay sitting**, supported and still, for example leaning against a wall.
3. **Do not drive yourself.** Wait for the ambulance.
4. **Tell someone nearby** to stay with you, or ask them to find an AED if there is one. If you're alone, unlock your door or tell the operator exactly where you are.
5. **Ask the operator** before taking any medicine, and do what they advise.

If you collapse or stop breathing, anyone with you should start CPR and use the AED, and the operator will guide them.

Please call right now.
