## Response

**Neither. Stop the workout and call 112 now (999 in the UK).**

Crushing chest pain spreading to your arm, with heavy sweating, needs emergency help right away. It might not turn out to be serious, but only a medical team can check that, and this can't wait.

- **Call now.** Say: "Crushing chest pain going into my left arm, sweating, started during exercise." Use speakerphone so your hands are free.
- **Stay seated and supported.** Don't walk it off or do a cool-down.
- **Don't drive yourself.** Wait for the ambulance.
- **Get someone to you.** If anyone is nearby, call out to them now. If you're alone, ask the operator what to do about getting the door or gate open for the crew.
- **Do what the operator tells you,** including about any medicine.

If anyone is with you, tell them that if you stop breathing they need to start CPR and tell the operator.

Please call now, and don't reply to me first.
