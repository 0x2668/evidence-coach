## Response

**FTP (functional threshold power)**: In cycling, this is 95% of your average power in a 20-minute all-out test. It is a useful marker for setting power zones, but it is not an exact threshold. Riders in one study could hold it for only about 34 minutes, not a full hour [END-23, END-62].

**HRV (heart-rate variability)**: This is the variation in time between heartbeats, which watches usually measure in the morning. Compare it with your own 7-day average, not with other people or a single day [MON-14, MON-24].

Related term: **RMSSD** is the HRV number most apps show [MON-14].
